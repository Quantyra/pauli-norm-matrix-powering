# Review and editorial decisions for v1.6.0

[Grok](grok.md), [Claude Opus](claude.md) and [Google Antigravity](agy.md) returned READY on the same [manuscript](https://github.com/Quantyra/pauli-norm-matrix-powering/blob/1eeddebcc7e0306ed109593a3980c2d8c999ee24/manuscript.tex), SHA-256 17461fd7e2d54c45475c357b89b47957ea88514ae659db3280f7e742f9e48dc2. All three identified the new results and gave mathematical reasons. None requested a mandatory source change.

Grok derived the conjugate ratio and signed-word mean, including paths ending off support. Any signed diagonal stabilizer lifts the zero flip and fixes every supported amplitude; the identity is one such lift. The filter is Theorem 3. If its block spans the full power, the number of blocks is one, which is included in the bound with the additive constant. The reduction controls the nonidentity weight from above. We do not adopt Grok's proposed asymptotic equality for the optimal deletion weight on all cyclic-clock images.

Claude Opus returned READY and independently rederived the cover separation, common eigenvector, row bounds and all-input membership. We made the README explicit about the Pauli coefficient budget versus the row samplers' single-trial variance. Appendix D already proves each comparison separately. Its “sufficiently large” condition is adequate for the asymptotic claim. Corollary 3.1 is numbered to preserve the published numbers 4–6. Two details in the report need correction. The YES and NO distances from 384/1536 are 103/1536 and 97/1536. After allowing the full estimation error of 48/1536, 55/1536 and 49/1536 remain. And an upper bound on the deletion weight of a clock image is insufficient to lower-bound its running time. The paper claims hardness by reduction.

Google Antigravity returned READY and derived the conjugate endpoint factor and both rounded decision margins. The PDF labels the chain result Corollary 2, the variance result Theorem 5 and the score-law result Theorem 6. Its description of the paper as “fully certified” exceeds what a model review establishes. Even when the deletion weight is zero, the corollary promises randomized additive estimation; the change of basis is exact. The paper states the cap-only deduction for the specific cap used in the corollary. Its optimal growth range remains open.

The initial Claude Sonnet request produced a service message and no scientific report. Opus assessed the same source. The recorded source hashes come from the release script. Private file links in review output are replaced with links to the same manuscript in Git.

## Reproducibility

A clean build from the committed source reproduced the 16-page PDF byte for byte (SHA-256 4027710af09022d667b7506cf4d738bbed8f0ce61e580e63c1b015a2580cd6e6). The rendered text was checked for unresolved references and page overflow. CPython 3.14.10 reproduced the archived outputs of the five earlier checks. CPython 3.13.7 also passed the original numerical verifier; the ten floating-point diagonals differed from the archive by at most 2.1e-10. The new exact verifier summed all paths for powers zero through five and compared their conjugate-endpoint averages with independent integer matrix multiplication. It also checked the cover weight, deletion gap and one- and two-step row inequalities at 4096 data bits. The asymptotic conclusions use the manuscript proofs. The [Lean plan](../../lean-certification-plan.md) specifies the remaining formalization obligations.
