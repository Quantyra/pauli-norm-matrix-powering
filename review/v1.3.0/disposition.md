The revised table identifies each placement from rules 1–6 of the original clock. Its two-site ranks sum to 16 inside a block, 14 on the marked bond and 12 at a block crossing. Rule 3 acts on every bond, so every disjoint pair used in the test word contributes. The verifier checks these ranks and placements across its twelve installations. The manuscript also spells out the order of inverse gates and the number of padding gates. The circuit comparison is asymptotic and requires no numerical crossover. [Aharonov, Gottesman, Irani and Kempe, §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.4077#page=11)

The normalized trace calculation in Corollary 4 gives an upper bound. The alternating word is the original witness with its left-moving flag labeled $A$ in the table. For Theorem 3 the dyadic masses can require a number of bits of order the power times the input precision, which is polynomial in the written length. The filter lower bound covers every polynomial uniformly accurate on the interval, including degrees greater than the target power. Its conclusion concerns the sum of absolute monomial coefficients weighted by the actual Pauli norm. This controls the stated sampler's prescribed trial budget. The paper leaves open what a variance-sensitive or state-dependent algorithm could achieve.

The sign-walk expansion of a monomial and the classical sampler both have prior sources. This revision combines them with blocking, proves the converse weighted coefficient bound, and evaluates the resulting guarantee on the rounded spin chains of Corollary 2. The reviews establish no claim of priority. [Sachdeva and Vishnoi, §3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.4882) · [Cifuentes et al., Lemma 19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13937#page=14)
